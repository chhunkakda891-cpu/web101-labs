Numbanhjok

Ingedient

  • Freshwater Fish: Used to create the savory gravy.
  • Fermented Rice Noodles: The specific thin noodles for which the dish is named.
  • Kroeung: The yellow aromatic paste made from lemongrass and turmeric
  • Fingerroot (Krachai): Provides the unique earthy flavor required for this fish soup.
  • Prahok: Fermented fish paste used for deep umami seasoning.

Cooking step

  1. Simmer the fish until cooked, remove the bones, and pound the meat into a paste.
  2. Create a yellow paste by pounding lemongrass, turmeric, galangal, garlic, and fingerroot.
  3. Fry the Kroeung paste and Prahok until fragrant, then stir in the fish meat.
  4. : Add water or broth to the pot and bring to a simmer (add coconut milk if desired).
